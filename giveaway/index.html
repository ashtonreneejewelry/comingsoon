No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not improve your chances. This giveaway is a random drawing, not a writing contest. Void where prohibited.

During the entry period, comment on the original giveaway post on the ASHTON RENÉE Facebook Page or Instagram account @ashtonreneejewelry. Tell us about a woman who inspires you and why. A brief comment is enough. Please do not include private contact information or sensitive personal details. Each person receives one entry total across Facebook and Instagram. Multiple comments or accounts do not create extra chances. If a person enters more than once, the earliest valid entry is retained. The sponsor will randomly select one potential winner on October 21, 2026, from the combined list of eligible, unique Facebook and Instagram entrants. Each eligible person has an equal chance. Odds depend on the number of eligible unique entrants. The sponsor will document the drawing and contact the potential winner privately through the official ASHTON RENÉE account on the platform used to enter.

The potential winner must respond within 72 hours of the first notification and privately confirm eligibility, two design selections and one U.S. shipping address. If the person is ineligible, declines, or does not respond within 72 hours, the sponsor may select an alternate by another random drawing. October 21 is the planned public announcement date, subject to winner verification. If verification takes longer, the announcement will follow once complete.

The sponsor will ship both prize bracelets together within 14 calendar days after eligibility, design selections and the shipping address are confirmed. Tracking will be provided privately. Delivery time is additional to preparation time. The sponsor will never require payment, card details or banking information to claim this prize.

Entries must be made by a real person through an authentic account. Automated, fraudulent, abusive or otherwise ineligible entries may be excluded. If a technical issue or fraud prevents fair administration, the sponsor may pause or cancel the giveaway and announce the change on the official accounts. If a drawing remains possible, it will use eligible entries received before the interruption. The sponsor is not responsible for entries that are lost or inaccessible because of platform outages or privacy settings.

Entry records will be used to administer the giveaway, verify eligibility, deliver the prize and retain giveaway records. The verified winner’s account handle may be announced publicly. Mailing addresses and other private details will not be published. Separate permission will be requested before featuring an entrant’s story or photograph in marketing. Entry does not enroll anyone in an email list.
