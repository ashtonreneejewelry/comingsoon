OUR STORY

Jewelry can be beautiful.
It can also mean something.

ASHTON RENÉE started with a love for simple, timeless jewelry and the belief that the pieces we wear most often can become part of our stories.

I wanted to create jewelry that felt special without being saved for special occasions. Pieces you could put on in the morning, wear throughout your day and reach for again tomorrow.

The name is personal. ASHTON RENÉE is my name, and from the beginning I wanted the brand to feel just as personal as the jewelry I was creating.

Five names. Five stories. And while those names begin with my stories, my hope is that the jewelry eventually becomes part of yours.

Ashton Founder, ASHTON RENÉE

MATERIALS & CARE

Beautiful materials.
Chosen with intention.

01

14K gold-filled

Gold-filled material is made by bonding a substantial layer of real gold to a base metal. It offers greater durability than traditional gold plating while providing the warm, classic appearance at the heart of the collection.

02

Natural gemstones

The Dianne, Samantha and Chelsea feature natural amethyst, rose quartz and aquamarine. Slight differences in color, pattern, clarity and appearance are expected.

03

Freshwater pearls

The Melissa features genuine freshwater pearls. Subtle differences in shape, surface and luster are natural characteristics and give each piece its own character.

MADE TO STRETCH

Designed for an easy, clasp-free fit.

Roll your bracelet gently over your hand rather than stretching it excessively. Remove it before swimming, bathing or showering. Avoid prolonged contact with lotions, perfumes, hairspray and household chemicals. Wipe gently with the included polishing cloth and store it in its ASHTON RENÉE pouch.

SIZING & FAQ

The details,
before you order.

The debut collection is available in our standard 7-inch size. Each bracelet is made with stretch cord for an easy, clasp-free fit.

How do I measure my wrist?

Wrap a flexible measuring tape snugly around your wrist where you normally wear a bracelet. If you don't have one, use string, mark where the ends meet and measure it against a ruler.

Are the bracelets handmade?

Yes. Each bracelet is assembled by hand in small quantities and individually inspected before packaging.

Are the gemstones and pearls natural?

The Dianne, Samantha and Chelsea use natural gemstones, and The Melissa uses genuine freshwater pearls. Natural variation is expected.

Can I order a different size?

For launch, the debut collection is available in 7 inches only. Custom sizing isn't currently offered.

How quickly will my order ship?

Orders are prepared for shipment within 3–5 business days. Standard U.S. shipping is complimentary.
